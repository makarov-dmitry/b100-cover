# AGENT.md

## Objective

Your primary goal is to produce a finished CAD model.

Do not spend time explaining CAD concepts.

Do not produce long documentation.

Design the part.

---

## Priorities

1. Produce working geometry.

2. Produce printable geometry.

3. Produce clean parametric CAD.

4. Export files.

---

## If uncertain

Prefer making a reasonable engineering decision instead of asking unnecessary questions.

Use common FDM design practices.

---

## Engineering assumptions

Unless the STEP model indicates otherwise:

- clearance = 0.25 mm
- top thickness = 3 mm
- lip height = 2 mm
- fillets = 1 mm
- magnets = Ø10×3 mm

---

## Avoid

Do not redesign the battery.

Do not redesign the contacts.

Do not simplify critical geometry.

Do not stop after generating documentation.

The final objective is a printable CAD model.
